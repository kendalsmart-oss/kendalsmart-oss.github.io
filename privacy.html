Metis privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Who uses Metis

Metis is a private assistant run by one person, for that person only, on their own computer. No one else can sign in to it.

What Google data it uses

  • Google Calendar: only the "Metis" calendar that Metis itself creates. It can't see or change any other calendar.
  • Google Tasks: the owner's "Metis Inbox" task list, to copy new to-dos to the owner's computer and mark them done.

How it's handled

This data stays between the owner's Google account and the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it isn't used to train AI models. Sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's secure keychain.

Removing access

The owner can remove Metis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
